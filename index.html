Step-by-Step

How to Use the Calculator

  1. Select US Customary or Metric from the Calculation Units dropdown.
  2. Enter the quantity delivered for the concrete load.
  3. Enter cement, fly ash, and other supplementary cement material.
  4. Enter plant water and any additional site water.
  5. Review the Water-Cement Ratio and Mix Summary values.
  6. Enter the project name in Report Details.
  7. Optionally open the Mix Design and Report tab to enter the mix design and any third-party test results provided.
  8. Attach the ticket photo so it can be included in the PDF report.
  9. Confirm the logged location, then print or save the combined report as a PDF.
